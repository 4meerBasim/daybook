# Weekly Spread Review

Status: planned
Summary: A weekly spread read as a balance sheet — committed against settled, tally marks per day, and a heat read of when work actually happens.

## Notes
The honest alternative to a streak counter. Streaks punish; a ledger just records. The week reads as a balance: what was committed, what was settled, what was carried, what was struck. Over a few weeks it tells the user something no todo app tells them — that Thursdays are fiction, or that they commit thirty percent more than they finish.

[[Calendar Tally Marks]] already establishes the visual language for workload per day, so the spread aggregates a shipped idea rather than inventing a new one.

Open decisions:

- Needs closed-page history to aggregate, so it depends on [[Closing the Page Ritual]] and on persistence.
- With estimates from [[Day Capacity Budget]] the balance can be stated in hours rather than counts, which is far more useful.
- Where it lives: a sibling route to [[Calendar]] or a mode of it. The design has no weekly view.
- A true two-page spread only works in landscape or on a tablet; on a phone it probably becomes one page with a rule down the middle.
